Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Unisphere Marketing ("Unisphere," "we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website at unispheremarketing.com (the "Site"). It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies — such as pixels, tags, and local storage — work in comparable ways. Together they help a website function, remember your preferences, and understand how it is used.

2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the Site, keep it secure, remember your preferences, measure performance, understand how visitors interact with our content, and support our marketing so we can improve our services and reach the right businesses.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential cookies

Required for the Site to function, including security, network management, and basic navigation. These cannot be switched off in our systems.

Performance & analytics cookies

Help us understand how visitors use the Site — for example, which pages are visited and how long visitors stay — so we can improve it. We may use tools such as Google Analytics.

Functional cookies

Remember your choices and preferences to provide enhanced, more personalized features.

Advertising & marketing cookies

Used to deliver relevant advertising and measure the performance of our campaigns, including through platforms such as Google Ads. These cookies may track your activity across websites.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third parties that provide services on our Site, including analytics and advertising platforms, our AI voice agent widget, and scheduling tools. We do not control these cookies. Please review the relevant third party's privacy and cookie policies for more information.

5. How Long Cookies Last

Session cookies are temporary and are deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain on your device until they expire or you delete them.

6. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Most browsers let you view, manage, delete, and block cookies through their settings. If you block essential cookies, parts of the Site may not work properly. You can also opt out of many advertising cookies through your Google Ads settings and through industry opt-out tools such as the Digital Advertising Alliance (optout.aboutads.info) and the Network Advertising Initiative (optout.networkadvertising.org).

7. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. Because there is no common industry standard for responding to DNT signals, our Site does not currently respond to them.

8. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted, as indicated by the "Last updated" date above.
